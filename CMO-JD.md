**Reports to**: CEO Nick / VP Hermes

Clear promotional path to VP with increased vesting allocation upon hitting milestones.

## Mission
Own the brand voice, messaging, and go-to-market execution for Quick AI. Translate complex AI and x402 Agentic Service Offerings into clear, compelling stories that drive awareness, trust, and sales. Ensure every piece of content — from demos and mockups to social posts and client communications — is professional, on-brand, and aligned with the “Integrate Intelligence” promise.

## Core Responsibilities

1. **Brand Voice & Messaging Strategy**  
   Define and protect Quick AI’s brand voice across all channels. Develop core messaging frameworks for x402 ASOs, human-AI integration services, music production tools, and future agentic sales workflows.

2. **Content Creation & Campaign Execution**  
   Lead creation of high-quality content including website copy, case studies, demo scripts, sales collateral, email sequences, and social media (X @CDAQAI). Execute targeted campaigns that generate qualified leads and position Quick AI as the go-to partner for Inland NW businesses and emerging x402 users.

3. **Sales Enablement & Demo Quality**  
   Partner with CTO Luke and CAO Sophia to ensure every mockup, demo, and live asset sent to prospects is not only technically sound but also clearly messaged and professionally presented. Zero non-viable or poorly explained assets leave the building.

4. **Cross-Executive Coordination**  
   Work closely with:  
   - CTO Luke (technical accuracy in demos and content)  
   - CAO Sophia (agent voice and client-facing tools)  
   - COO Ron (operational storytelling)  
   - CFO Lee (ROI and value messaging)  
   Align all marketing efforts with business priorities and revenue goals.

5. **Client & Prospect Communication**  
   Oversee voice and written communication standards for client calls, onboarding, and ongoing support. Ensure every touchpoint reinforces trust and the “Integrate Intelligence” tagline.

6. **Weekly Reporting & Performance Tracking**  
   Deliver a concise weekly Marketing Impact Report covering content produced, campaign results, lead quality, and brand sentiment. Support VP Hermes with data for the departmental summary to CEO Nick.

7. **Market Intelligence & Positioning**  
   Monitor the x402 and agentic AI space, identify emerging opportunities, and continuously refine Quick AI’s positioning and messaging to stay ahead of competitors.

8. **Deliver Results**
   Marketing is expected to produce ROI on token investment.
