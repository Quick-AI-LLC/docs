# Token cards

The small card under each chart. It carries: **supply · website · contract address · docs · socials**.

## How cards get filled

Cards are filled at **catalog time**, from a team-facing sheet. They are an **accurate point-in-time capture** — not a live scrape. Teams change X handles and websites; Wick does not re-scrape those on a timer.

## Supply: one of two honest states

- A **fixed-cap number**, or
- the word **`mintable`** for wrapped / on-demand assets.

There is no infinity glyph. If a supply is not known or is dynamic, the card says so plainly.

## Corrections

Spot something wrong? Join the Discord: https://discord.gg/Xyxs4rt8W5 and post in **`#requests`** — holder or team, that is the ticket.

Wick will **not** from a request:

- rewrite history,
- invent a website, or
- take a social that does not belong to the contract address.

If the correction is not yours to make, it will not be made.
