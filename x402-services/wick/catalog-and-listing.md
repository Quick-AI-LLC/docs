# Catalog and listing

Teams sometimes confuse "we're on the indexer" with "we're Listed on Wick." They are two different steps. This page separates them.

## Step 1 — Catalog

Wick indexes the pool. The series (15-minute snapshots, portable data) is Wick's from the first capture. Token-card fields are captured at this moment (see [Token cards](token-cards.md)).

**Cataloging is not an endorsement and not a listing.** It means Wick observes the pool and holds the data.

## Step 2 — List on Wick (Integrate)

A one-time fee. The flow:

1. Wick gives the team a bound receiving address.
2. Inbound USDC alerts the team.
3. The listing is verified and flipped to **Integrated**.
4. The asset becomes first-class on the interface: chart is front-and-center, plus cards, rails, and the share path.

**Catalog data does not depend on the fee.** Once cataloged, the series stays available until the LP is actually dead. Paying does not buy the history — the history is already Wick's.

## Pricing

**List on Wick is a flat one-time fee** — currently **$250 USDC**. It is the *current* price to list, with no escalator: any future change is announced in advance, never raised silently.

A **featured home slot** is also available to listed teams: a 3-day window in the default landing chart, starting at **$50 USDC**, marked "Promoted" (placement is not an endorsement). Its price tracks demand — it is the rotating exposure lane, not the base unlock.

## What the fee is not

- **Not a subscription.** Pay once.
- **Not a data-retention fee.** Your data is not held hostage.
- **Not a promise the asset stays on the interface forever** if the pool dies — that is the quality filter, covered on the [delisting](delisting.md) page.

## Founding and genesis assets

Assets that were cataloged through the Arc grant submission ship **fully Integrated** — full interface presence — so Wick launches with a real chartable suite rather than an empty rail and a fee wall.

They are subject to the **same** delisting and catalog-purge rules as any later paid listing. There is no exemption. Founding status reflects that Wick is the indexer of record for a chain that just launched — it is not a free pass, and it is not an endorsement.

The always-on core pairs — **WETH, cirBTC, EURC, CRCL, NVDA** — are the liquid LPs the habit is built on.
