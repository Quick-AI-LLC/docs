# FAQ

- **Is Wick a DexScreener for Arc?** No. It is an Arc-native terminal, quieter on purpose — built around Delta and a single-stable catalog.
- **Does cataloging cost anything?** No. Cataloging is free; it just means Wick observes the pool.
- **Does listing expire?** The fee is one-time. Interface presence lasts while the market is real — not a subscription, not a renewal.
- **If you delist us, do we lose the data?** No. Interface and warehouse are separate. History stays retrievable. See [Delisting](delisting.md).
- **What if we rug / the LP dies?** The interface drops it after an inactivity window; the series remains retrievable.
- **Why is our Twitter wrong?** Post in Discord `#requests` — that is the correction ticket. Cards are filled at catalog time, not scraped live.
- **Why is the bottom pane not volume?** That is Delta, not volume. [Delta](delta.md) explains why volume is the wrong glyph for a USDC-quoted LP.
- **Why USDC pairs only?** That is how Delta is expressed in one unit — it is also the founding catalog.
- **Can I get this as an API?** Not a published Wick route yet. See [Data access](data-access.md).
- **Where is Discord?** https://discord.gg/Xyxs4rt8W5
