# Using the chart

_As of 28 Sep 2026 — the live canvas on wick.green._

A first session on Wick without a tour video.

## Start

- Open wick.green. **Search** covers the full catalog.
- The **right rail** is a shortlist (Most Momentum / newly added) — it is not the universe.

## Navigate

- **Pan / zoom:** wheel to zoom, left-drag to pan.
- **Right-click** opens the Wick-specific context menu.

## The pane stack

Top-to-bottom:

1. **Price** (with overlays)
2. **Delta**
3. **Optional indicator pane**

## Applied technicals

Lines, zones, and shapes — with color and interior opacity controls in the on-site editor.

> **Note:** v1 persistence and shareable permalinks are a later milestone. Today your annotations live in the session; there is no share URL yet. We don't document a feature that hasn't shipped — check back after the permalink release.

## Publish

The publish bar groups: **note · PNG · PDF · Share to X**, as shown on the site.

## Cards under the chart

Suppy / website / contract address / docs / socials. To correct a card, see [Token cards](token-cards.md).

## Two terms

- **`mintable`** — for a wrapped or on-demand asset, supply is not a fixed number.
- **"undetermined"** — a field we couldn't honestly fill at capture.
