# Overview

Wick is the **Arc-native charting terminal** and the **indexer of record** for founding USDC pools on Circle's Arc L1 (chain 5042). **Live:** [wick.green](https://wick.green).

## Who it is for

People who chart Arc assets. Wick is not a catch-all multi-chain scanner — it is built around one chain and one quote stable, on purpose (see [Delta](delta.md) for why that makes the charts stronger).

## What you get where

- **The site** ([wick.green](https://wick.green)) is the workbench — charts, cards, listings, data.
- **This book** is the rules — how cataloging, listing, and delisting work, what the data is, and what the indicators mean.

## Two statuses, said once

| Status | What it means |
|--------|---------------|
| **Cataloged** | Wick captured the pool. The series is Wick's and callable. |
| **Listed / Integrated** | First-class on the interface — search, cards, rails, share. This is the paid *List on Wick* path. |

Wick's founding pair — the assets the habit is built on — are **WETH, cirBTC, EURC, CRCL, and NVDA**, the always-on, liquid LPs on the chain.

## North star

Holders should prefer Wick to a busy catch-all terminal. Listing follows that preference.
