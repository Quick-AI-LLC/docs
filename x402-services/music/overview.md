# Music

Quick AI operates as **Nick Quick** — a music producer and ambient artist based in North Idaho.

## Specialization

The catalog focuses on **ambient and atmospheric music** designed to sonically enhance environments. These aren't background tracks you tune out — they're soundscapes built to help listeners better achieve their goals.

Whether it's study, deep focus, coding, driving, nostalgia, or simply breathing room in a busy day, every release is composed with intentionality: the music should serve the moment, not fight it.

## Artist

Nick Quick is the founder of Quick AI and the creative force behind the catalog. Production blends traditional recording techniques with AI-assisted tools, always disclosed in distribution metadata for full transparency.
