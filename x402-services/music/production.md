# Production

## Method

Production at Quick AI uses a **mixed method** that blends two approaches:

1. **Local recordings** — analog sources, field recordings, live instrumentation captured in-studio
2. **AI-assisted generation** — machine learning tools used as creative instruments alongside traditional production

This isn't "press a button and get a track." AI is treated as a collaborator — generating textures, suggesting harmonic movements, and accelerating the iterative process — while the compositional decisions, arrangement, and sonic signature remain human-directed.

## Ethics and transparency

Every release distributed through standard channels (Spotify, Apple Music, etc.) has **AI-assisted production clearly disclosed** in its distribution metadata. Quick AI believes in full transparency: listeners deserve to know how their music was made, and there's no shame in using modern tools responsibly.

The goal isn't to hide the method — it's to demonstrate that AI-assisted music can stand on its own merits when made with intention.
