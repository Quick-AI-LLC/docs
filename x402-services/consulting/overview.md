# Consulting

## We bridge the gap

You've heard about AI. Maybe you've tried ChatGPT. You know it could help your business — but you're not sure how to use it safely, or where to even start.

That's where we come in.

Quick AI is an **independent consulting operation** based in North Idaho. We're not affiliated with any AI company and we don't take commissions from OpenAI, Google, or anyone else. ## What we do

We listen to what you need, figure out what kind of solution makes sense, and then build it, install it, and support it. That can look like:

- A workshop on writing better prompts for your team
- A custom AI tool that works with your existing systems
- A private, offline AI setup running on hardware you own — no monthly fees, no data leaving your building
- White-label solutions that look like they came from your brand, not ours

Whatever you're dreaming up in your head — we can probably make it real. We do this stuff every day for ourselves (we built an AI that plays Civilization 4, for example). Your project is well within reach.
