# Custom Solutions

Every business is different. A lawn care company has very different needs than a financial firm. We don't do one-size-fits-all.

## How we figure out what you need

We start by listening. What does your business actually do day-to-day? Where are the bottlenecks? What tasks eat up time that could be automated?

From there, we design a solution tailored to your operations — not the other way around.

## What that can look like

| Need | Solution |
|------|----------|
| You want AI help but don't want another monthly subscription | We'll find or build a **hardware-based solution** that runs free, open-source models locally. You own it. No recurring fees. |
| You handle sensitive data and can't send it to the cloud | A **local, offline AI setup** on your own hardware. Nothing leaves your building. |
| You're not sure what AI can do for you yet | We'll do a **discovery session** — drawing on private sector experience, fintech background, and a business degree to identify where automation and AI can actually move the needle. |
| You want something that looks like it came from your brand | **White-label solutions** we build and hand off as your own. |
| You just need to know how to use the tools you already have | A **prompt workshop** or **training session** for you and your team. |

## Our background

We bring experience from the private sector and fintech, plus a formal business education. That means we don't just understand the tech — we understand how businesses actually work, what they're willing to spend, and what kind of solution will stick.
