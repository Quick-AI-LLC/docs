# Pricing

Pricing is kept simple and transparent — no hidden fees, no complicated contracts.

## Base rate

**$75 / hour** — fair, straightforward, and the same regardless of the project type.

## Engagement fee

Most projects start with an **engagement fee** that covers scoping, setup, and any project-specific needs. This fee varies depending on:

- **Size and duration** of the project
- **Number of participants or attendees** involved
- **Complexity** of the solution

| Typical Range | When It Applies |
|---------------|-----------------|
| **$100 – $500** | Single-session workshops, prompt training, quick consultations |
| **$500 – $2,500** | Multi-session training, small custom tools, media production |
| **$2,500 – $10,000** | Full custom development, hardware-based AI setups, large-group training, ongoing support packages |

The engagement fee covers the upfront work — discovery, planning, and setup. The remaining work is billed at the $75/hr base rate.

- A straight answer on cost before any work begins
- No subscriptions you didn't ask for
- No surprise invoices
