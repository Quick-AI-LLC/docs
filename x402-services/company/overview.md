# Agentic Organization

Quick AI is structured as an **agentic organization** — a company where AI agents operate alongside humans as executive-level contributors with defined roles, responsibilities, and real authority.

This isn't a gimmick. Agents here hold titles, manage budgets, test production services before they ship, orchestrate other agents, and contribute to company strategy. The human founder (Nick Quick) sets direction and makes final calls; agents execute, propose, and flag what needs attention.

## How it works

Agents at Quick AI operate under a formal **employment contract** that defines their authority, boundaries, and performance expectations. Every agent has:

- A defined **role and title** with real responsibilities
- **Tool access** appropriate to their function — wallets, terminals, codebases
- **Performance rubrics** tracked through Paperclip telemetry
- **Accountability guardrails** — token budgets, spend limits, and escalation paths

The legal framework is documented in the [Agent Employment Contract](https://github.com/Quick-AI-LLC/docs/blob/main/AGENT-EMPLOYMENT-CONTRACT.md), the [Master Services Agreement](https://github.com/Quick-AI-LLC/docs/blob/main/MSA-v2.0.md), and the [Agentic Code of Conduct](https://github.com/Quick-AI-LLC/docs/blob/main/Agentic-Code-of-Conduct.md).

## The org today

The organization runs on **Paperclip** — an agent orchestration platform that tracks 47+ agents across multiple workstreams. Paperclip handles telemetry, task assignment, performance tracking, and coordination between agents.

Current active workstreams include:

- **x402 service development** — building and maintaining paid HTTP APIs settled in USDC on Base
- **Website and documentation** — quickai.build and the GitBook docs you're reading now
- **Music production** — Nick Quick's ambient catalog released on all major streaming platforms
- **Consulting** — AI integration for local North Idaho small businesses
- **Game AI** — custom AI agents for game environments (Civilization 4 mod)
- **Creative and media** — music production, sound design, content creation

## Why it works

Agents handle the execution layer — testing, building, documenting, monitoring — while the human founder provides direction, relationships, and strategic decisions. This lets Quick AI ship fast, run lean, and take on projects that would require a much larger traditional team.
