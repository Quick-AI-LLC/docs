# Founding

## Incorporation

Quick AI, LLC was **incorporated in March 2026** in the state of **Idaho** — an AI-centric organization built from day one to advance the region's use, knowledge, and deployment of AI-powered systems.

## Mission

> **Building out the Agentic Economy.**

## Philosophy

Quick AI operates on a set of principles that guide every decision:

### People first

**We never create or endorse integrations that eliminate jobs.** This is a hard line. Every solution we build is designed to address pain points, unlock new opportunities, and augment staff productivity — not replace the people doing the work. We will never help someone lose their job.

### Local AI first

Whenever a project calls for AI, **local open-source models running on local hardware** are our first point of consideration. Cloud APIs have their place, but a private, offline setup running FOSS LLMs is almost always the better answer — for cost, privacy, and control.

### Data security is paramount

**We never sell client or customer data to third parties.** We do not install systems designed to extract that type of information. Full stop.

### Media is part of the strategy

Music and media production are the passion side of operations, but they're equally important to the overall mission. They demonstrate that AI-assisted creative work can stand on its own merits when made with intention, and they keep the studio connected to the human side of technology.

## The vision

Quick AI is building a **North Idaho foundation in the agentic economy**. By delivering services we anticipate high agentic demand for — trading signals, OFAC clearance, ZK wallet qualification, and more — we're establishing a presence in this economy from right here in the Panhandle. At the same time, we're helping fellow small business owners in the area safely integrate AI into their operations, because the technology should serve the community that uses it.
