## 1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, such as when you contact us, agree to a SOW, or engage our Services (e.g., business contact details, Client Data you share for RAG Knowledge Base Setup, custom agentic builds, or organizational integrations). We do not collect personal information from your end customers unless you explicitly provide it as part of the Services.

For x402 transactions, we do **not** collect or store any personal information. Blockchain transactions are processed by the Client directly via public blockchain networks. The only data visible is the wallet address, transaction amount, and timestamp — all of which are publicly visible on the blockchain by design.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use Client Data solely to deliver and support the Services you requested, nothing more. This includes setup, training, revisions (as outlined in your SOW), and limited backup/support during the paid Support Period.

## 3. No Selling of Data – Ever

Quick AI will **never** sell your information, your company's data, or any data belonging to your customers to advertisers, data brokers, or any third party for marketing purposes. Period. We do not monetize data.

Some of our Services may involve integrating with third-party cloud AI providers (e.g., Google Gemini, Microsoft Copilot, or similar). Once your data leaves our systems and enters those platforms, we have no control over it. Those providers have their own privacy policies, data practices, and security measures. You are responsible for reviewing them and deciding whether they meet your needs. As noted in Section 3 of our MSA, you acknowledge the inherent risks of data leaks or unauthorized access when using cloud AI services, and Quick AI bears no liability for any issues arising from those third-party platforms.

---

## 5. x402 Payments & Blockchain Transparency

x402 Services are paid via public blockchain networks (e.g., Base, Polygon) using USDC or other digital assets. All on-chain transactions are **public by design**. Wallet addresses, transaction amounts, and timestamps are visible to anyone with access to the blockchain. Quick AI does not collect, store, or have access to your private keys, seed phrases, or wallet credentials. You are solely responsible for securing your own wallet and ensuring the accuracy of any transaction you authorize.

---

## 6. Data Retention

For services that involve us handling your documents or data (such as RAG Knowledge Base Setup or custom workflow automation), we retain Client Data only for 6–12 months after project completion solely for backup and support purposes to be used during any active paid Support Period. All such data is stored securely on encrypted systems. After the retention window, we digitally shred (permanently delete) the data using industry-standard secure deletion methods. We do not sell, share, or repurpose this data in any way.

---

## 7. Our Preference: Local AI for Maximum Privacy Control

At Quick AI, our ideal approach is local AI installations. Local setups give you total ownership and control over your data — no third-party cloud involvement, no external access. You decide exactly who can see or use your information.

That said, even local AI is not a complete "silver bullet" for privacy. If your agentic systems interact with customers or the internet (e.g., crypto trading bots on Polygon or any public-facing automations), sophisticated trickery, hacking attempts, or social-engineering tactics could still expose data. We always advise clients to layer additional safeguards (firewalls, access controls, monitoring) and to treat local AI as the far superior privacy option compared to cloud-only solutions — while still staying vigilant.
